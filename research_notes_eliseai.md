## Source 7: EliseAI Support Documentation - Calendar and Tours Settings

### Tour Types Supported
1. **In-Person Tours** - Traditional agent-led tours
2. **Self-Guided Tours** - Prospect tours independently
3. **Live Virtual Tours** - Video call tours via Whereby integration

### Tour Prioritization
- Drag and drop tour types to set priority order
- AI Assistant tries to book highest priority tour type first

### Booking Methods
1. **In-Conversation**: AI offers times and books directly in chat
2. **Link**: AI provides external scheduling link for self-service
3. **Whereby** (virtual only): AI books and sends video meeting link

### Key Configuration Options

#### Appointment Duration
- Set in 15-minute intervals (15, 30, 45, etc.)
- Can add buffer time between tours

#### Valid Appointment Start Times
- On the 15 (:00, :15, :30, :45)
- On the 30 (:00, :30)
- Top of the hour only (:00)

#### Advanced Notice for Appointment
- Minimum time before first available slot (in minutes)
- Example: 120 minutes = 2 hours advance notice required
- Can book up to 30 days in advance

#### Concurrency Settings
- **Different Tour Types**: Can run multiple tour types simultaneously
- **Same Tour Type**: Set max concurrent appointments per time slot
- Example: Set to 2 = up to 2 appointments per slot

#### Simultaneous Touring (In-Person Only)
- Allows multiple in-person tours for same agent's time slot
- Multiple prospects can tour property at same time

### Organization-Wide vs Community-Level Settings
- **Organization-Wide**: Holiday settings apply to ALL communities
- **Community-Level**: Tour settings, office hours customized per property

### Holiday Management
- Toggle US public holidays on/off
- Create custom holidays
- AI will not schedule tours on enabled holidays

### Leasing Office Hours
- Set different hours per tour type
- Example: In-person until 5 PM, self-guided until 8 PM

### Self-Guided Tour Access
- Can configure access issue instructions for prospects
- Integrates with smart locks/lockboxes for access codes

## Source 9: EliseAI Blog - Getting the Most Out of Your AI Agent (Handoff & Training)

### AI Agent Training Philosophy
- Treat AI Agent like a new employee
- AI is only as good as the training and information provided
- Requires ramp-up period like any new hire

### Knowledge Bank System
- AI pulls information from Property Management Software (PMS) and supplemental Knowledge Bank
- Full visibility into what AI does and doesn't know
- Onsite team members should input supplementary knowledge

### Key Training Topics to Input
- Company-level knowledge
- Property-level knowledge
- Fair housing guidelines
- Rental requirements
- Screening requirements
- Application screening requirements
- Income requirements for approvals
- Acceptable forms of income
- Housing voucher acceptance/management
- Corporate-approved responses to common questions

### Pending Knowledge Review
- Review "Pending Knowledge" daily during ramp-up
- AI flags questions it doesn't have answers for
- Like a new hire asking for clarification
- Approving pending knowledge improves automation over time

### Key Performance Metrics

#### Handoff Percentage
- % of conversations AI hands off to human because it lacks knowledge
- **High-performing teams: ~5% handoff rate** (AI handles 95% autonomously)
- If over 10%, AI is missing key information in Knowledge Bank

#### Early Takeover Rate
- % of conversations where human took over when AI didn't need help
- **High-performing teams: under 5%**
- Too many early takeovers prevent AI from learning

### Handoff Best Practices

#### Standard Operating Procedures
- Update leasing policies around handoffs
- Clear instructions: should handoff prompt phone call, text, or email?
- Don't leave to agent discretion - creates inconsistency

#### Accountability
- Designate specific person responsible for handoffs
- Make it key part of their responsibilities
- Consider making them responsible for reviewing Pending Knowledge daily
- "Onboarding buddy" concept - one experienced team member manages AI

### Risk of Poor Handoff Management
- Delayed response = negative resident interaction
- Missed opportunity to convert new lead
- Inconsistent customer experiences

## Source 10: EliseAI VoiceAI Emergency Escalation Behavior

### Two Escalation Options

#### 1. Live Call Escalation
- Keeps resident on the line
- AI calls maintenance technicians in emergency routing plan
- Caller hears hold music during escalation
- If first technician misses call, AI tells resident team was notified and continues dialing

#### 2. Message Relay Escalation
- AI plays message: "We will be escalating this issue to our emergency maintenance team immediately, and you will receive a text or call back soon."
- AI ends the call
- AI starts calling the routing plan

### Call Answering Options (for Technicians)

#### Live Call Response Options
- Press 1: Connect with resident and accept the issue
- Press 2: De-escalate as routine maintenance
- Press 3: Report a known issue
- Press 0: Replay the message

#### Message Relay Response Options
- Press 1: Acknowledge issue and receive summary
- Press 2: De-escalate as routine maintenance
- Press 3: Report a known issue
- Press 0: Replay the message

### Text Notifications to Technicians
Information included:
- Escalation Number
- Status: Accepted, Pending, De-escalated, Known Issue
- Resident Name
- Unit Number
- Details of the issue
- Conversation Link (for callback, masks phone number)
- Contact Info
- Name of person who accepted (if applicable)

### Escalation Workflow
1. Accepting escalation stops the call path (no more calls to others)
2. "Escalation accepted" text sent to those previously notified
3. Team member should reply to acknowledge
4. Confirmation text sent: "Escalation #206 accepted/de-escalated/reported known by {name}"

### Unrecognized Residents
- AI will initiate emergency call routing
- Will NOT create work order
- Prioritizes emergency escalation over resident authentication

### Resident Messages After Resolution
- **Accepted**: "Your emergency maintenance request has been accepted, and someone will be on their way shortly!"
- **De-escalated**: "A maintenance technician has reviewed it and determined it's not an emergency under our guidelines. Our team will handle it as routine maintenance during office hours."
- **Known Issue**: "The maintenance team is already aware of the issue and will reach out for more information if needed."
