## Source 3: Digital Labor Blog Post (by Haoyu Zha, CEO)

### Definition of Digital Labor
**Digital labor** = deployment of **agentic AI** inside community association management workflows
- AI agents plan tasks
- Make policy-aware decisions
- Complete multi-step processes with human review as needed
- Unlike basic chatbots that only answer questions

### Key Differentiator
"Go beyond chat. Get work done." - Agents don't just answer questions, they:
- Read invoices
- Code to GL
- Route for approval
- Build draft budgets
- Answer billing questions
- Create action items automatically

### Coverage Model: Front Office + Back Office
- **Front Office**: Voice, web, and SMS handle homeowner requests in real time
- **Back Office**: Agents process accounting and management workflows

### Human-in-the-Loop Design
- Start in supervised mode
- Measure accuracy
- Then increase autonomy as confidence grows

### Specific Agent Types Described

#### 1. Homeowner Voice Agent
- Answers calls
- Authenticates the caller
- Checks balances
- Follows late-fee policy
- Opens work orders
- Documents the interaction

#### 2. AP Agent (Accounts Payable)
- Extracts invoice data
- Matches vendor and association
- Codes line items
- Flags exceptions
- Routes for approval

#### 3. Budget Agent
- Pulls historicals and reserves
- Proposes a policy-aligned draft
- Outputs notes for board review

### Real Customer Outcomes

#### Silverleaf Management Group
- ~250 associations and ~25,000 doors
- HOAi Voice handles ~1,400 inbound calls/week
- Average wait time reduced from ~4 minutes to seconds
- No staff reductions; improved experience

#### Katonah Management
- Win rate on board presentations increased from 25% → 75%
- Drivers: ticketing, transparent reporting, technology

### Implementation Timeline (30-60-90 Days)
- **Days 1-30**: Pick one high-volume workflow (AP). Baseline KPIs and enable supervised mode
- **Days 31-60**: Track acceptance rate and cycle-time gains. Expand to second workflow
- **Days 61-90**: Share wins, tune policies, plan phased move to more autonomy

### FAQ Insights
- "Will AI agents replace our team?" - No. Digital labor handles repetitive work so people can focus on relationships, strategy, and exceptions
- "Agents sit above your existing stack and act like knowledgeable coworkers within your workflows"

- 75% of new housing developments are built within these communities
- $103 billion collected annually in homeowner assessments

### Key Technical Architecture Insights

#### AI-First Design Philosophy
Ben Currin (CEO): "We've shifted from UI-first and API-first to AI-first. Everything our software does now must also work for AI agents, not just for people. That means designing every workflow so that a digital coworker can pick it up, understand context, and execute it end-to-end."

#### Agentic AI Definition (from Haoyu Zha, HOAi Founder)
"Agentic AI is where AI moves from generating answers to getting work done. It's about using generative intelligence to reason through context, make judgments, and create real-life impact."

#### Digital Coworker Model
- Not just task automation - knowledge-worker agents that can reason, make judgments, and operate independently
- Agents understand context, learn from results, engage in workflows as collaborative team members
- "The real breakthrough isn't automation, it's augmentation"

### Four Key Imperatives for Agentic AI Success

1. **Rethink Software Bottoms Up**
   - Move up the software value stack
   - True differentiation in application/workflow layer
   - Link reasoning models with real-world actions

2. **Go Vertical In Nuanced Markets**
   - Focus on knowledge work, not just task automation
   - Domain-tuned agentic systems for industries with high labor spend
   - "The value is in the business layer—the unique workflows, interfaces, and decisions"

3. **Target Labor Spend, Not IT Budgets**
   - Labor costs exceed 50% of operating costs in most industries
   - Traditional enterprise software only 2-3% of business spending
   - "Agentic AI opens up a 10× bigger market"

4. **Create Trust Between Humans and Agents**
   - "We think of ourselves as an HR company providing digital workers"
   - Help leaders manage AI agents like people
   - Measure performance, build confidence, improve over time

### Architecture Differentiators vs Legacy Systems
- AI-native from ground up (vs. rules-based workflows)
- Intelligent agents drive knowledge work (vs. rule-based triggers)
- Agents reason, adapt, coordinate across modules
- Compositional, dynamically adaptable, learning-based
- New use cases managed by agents leveraging prior experience, shared context, feedback loops

- 1 million+ agentic tasks completed

- $250 million estimated customer cost savings
- Silverleaf: 600% portfolio growth, 4,000+ tasks in 60 days, 400+ hours saved
- Phone wait times: 4 minutes → 3 seconds
- A/P automation: 95% automated, invoices accelerated by 90%
