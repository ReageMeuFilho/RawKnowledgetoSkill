## 🆕 Unique Skills (New to Registry)

### 1. SKILL-207: integrated-banking-platform

**Priority**: P1
**Category**: fintech

**Description**: 
Full banking services (checking + savings) integrated directly into property management platform.

**Key Features**:
- Unlimited checking accounts
- Unlimited savings accounts
- Physical + virtual debit cards
- ACH transfers (internal instant, external 1-3 days)
- Wire transfers
- Spend controls on cards
- FDIC insured up to $250K

**Why Revolutionary**:
- No other PM platform offers **actual banking**
- Eliminates need for separate bank accounts
- All property finances in one place
- Rent deposits directly into platform accounts

**Business Model Insight**: Baselane earns on float from deposits + premium subscriptions.

### 3. SKILL-209: schedule-e-auto-categorization

**Priority**: P0
**Category**: tax-automation

**Description**: 
Automatic categorization of transactions to IRS Schedule E line items for rental property tax reporting.

**Schedule E Categories Built-In**:
- **Revenue**: Rents, Application fees, Late fees, Pet fees
- **Expenses**: Advertising, Auto & Travel, Cleaning & Maintenance, Commissions, Depreciation, Insurance, Legal & Professional Fees, Management Fees, Repairs, Supplies, Taxes, Utilities

**How It Works**:
1. Transaction imported from bank/card
2. AI suggests Schedule E category
3. User confirms or corrects
4. Rules learned for future auto-tagging
5. Tax reports pre-populated

**Why Critical**: 
- **US landlord pain point #1** = tax preparation
- Schedule E alignment means accountant-ready data
- Eliminates manual categorization

### 4. SKILL-210: property-account-association

**Priority**: P0
**Category**: fintech

**Description**: 
Link specific bank accounts to specific properties for automatic cash flow tracking and separation.

**Account Types per Property**:
| Association | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| Operating Account | Rent deposits, expenses |
| Security Deposit Account | Tenant deposits (legally required separation in many states) |
| Reserve Account | Capital expenditure savings |

**Why Critical**:
- **Legal requirement** in many states to separate security deposits
- Automatic property-level P&L
- Clean audit trail per property
- Easy owner reporting

### 7. SKILL-213: tenant-screening-integrated

**Priority**: P1
**Category**: tenant-management

**Description**: 
Comprehensive background checks for prospective tenants via TransUnion SmartMove.

**Screening Reports**:
| Report Type | Contents |
|-------------|----------|

| Criminal Background | Criminal history search |
| Eviction History | Prior eviction records |

**Workflow**:
