## 🆕 Unique Skills (New to Registry)

### 1. SKILL-224: flexible-service-tiers

**Priority**: P0
**Category**: hybrid-pm

**Description**: 
Ability to switch between service tiers at any time, with different tiers assignable to different properties.

**Tier Structure**:
| Tier | Monthly | Features |
|------|---------|----------|
| Basic | ~$2.50/unit | Core software, DIY |
| Essential | $24/unit | + Leasing & maintenance coordination |
| Complete | $80/unit | + Full-service, tenant communication |

**Key Differentiators**:
- Switch anytime (pro-rated)
- Different tiers per property
- No long-term contracts
- Monthly or annual billing

**Why Revolutionary**: Traditional PM = all-or-nothing. Hemlane = **pick your level**.

### 3. SKILL-226: eviction-shield-protection

**Priority**: P0
**Category**: risk-management

**Description**: 
Insurance-like service ($4.95/unit/month) that handles delinquent tenant situations.

**Three-Step Process**:
1. **Mediation**: Contact tenant, create payment plan (**90% resolution rate**)
2. **Legal Notices**: Local process server delivers three notices
3. **Attorney Connection**: Connect with local attorney for formal proceedings

**Why Revolutionary**:
- "Apple Care for your rental"
- Removes emotional burden from landlords
- Professional handling of difficult situations
- 90% resolved without eviction

**Key Insight**: Most landlords dread evictions. This removes the fear.

### 7. SKILL-230: split-allocation-income

**Priority**: P1
**Category**: financial-management

**Description**: 
Automatic distribution of rental income to multiple parties based on predefined rules.

**Use Cases**:
- **Co-owners**: Split rent 50/50 to different accounts
- **Partnerships**: Distribute by ownership percentage
- **Property Managers**: Auto-allocate management fee
- **Investors**: Separate returns to different investors

**How It Works**:
1. Define allocation rules (% or fixed amounts)
2. Rent collected through platform
3. Automatic distribution to designated accounts
4. Full audit trail

**Why Valuable**: Eliminates manual splitting, reduces disputes.

### Key Insight for Our Platform

Hemlane validates the **hybrid model**:
- Landlords want **control AND help** simultaneously
- **A-la-carte services** = new revenue streams
- **Tier flexibility** = lower churn (85-90% retention)
- **In-house teams** = quality differentiation

For our platform:
- Consider **tiered service model** like Hemlane
- **Eviction support** as add-on service
- **Turnover services** as productized offering
- **Agent network** for local presence
