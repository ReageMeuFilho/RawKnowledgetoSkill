## 🎯 Strategic Priorities

### Approach A: Full Hybrid Model

**Strategy**: Build complete Hemlane-like hybrid model.

**Benefits**:
- Higher retention (85-90%)
- Multiple revenue streams
- Competitive differentiation
- Serve broader market

**Requirements**:
- Tier infrastructure
- In-house coordination team
- Service partner network
- Operational complexity

**Investment**: HIGH

---

### Approach B: Software + A-La-Carte

**Strategy**: Core software + optional services.

**Benefits**:
- Lower operational complexity
- Revenue from services
- Flexibility for landlords
- Simpler to start

**Requirements**:
- Core platform
- Service partnerships
- Clear pricing

**Investment**: MEDIUM

---

### Approach C: Software-Only + Integrations

**Strategy**: Stay software-only, integrate with service providers.

**Benefits**:
- Lowest complexity
- Focus on software excellence
- Partner leverage

**Requirements**:
- Integration partnerships
- Clear positioning

**Investment**: LOW

**Recommendation**: Start with **Approach B** - software + selected a-la-carte services (eviction protection, turnover) - then expand to full hybrid if successful.
