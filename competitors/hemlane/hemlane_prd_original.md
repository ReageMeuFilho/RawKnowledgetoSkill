## 1. Introduction

Hemlane is a hyper-growing company servicing over **30,000 units** across the United States, with a user base of approximately 7,000. The platform boasts a high customer retention rate of **85-90%**, positioning itself as a leading "alternative to traditional property management."

The platform unbundles services, allowing investors to choose their level of involvement - from fully DIY to fully managed.

---

## 2. Key Differentiators from Traditional Property Management

| Feature | Hemlane | Traditional Property Management |
|---------|---------|--------------------------------|
| **Fee Structure** | Flat-rate monthly fee per unit | Percentage of gross rent (6-10%) |
| **Flexibility** | Switch tiers anytime, different packages per property | Fixed-term agreements with cancellation fees |
| **Transparency** | Full visibility into operations, communications, tickets | "Black box" with limited owner visibility |
| **Repair Costs** | No markups on vendor invoices | 10-20% surcharge on maintenance |
| **Control** | Owners approve repairs over threshold | Managers make most decisions |
| **Contracts** | Monthly or annual, changeable/cancelable | Long-term contracts with penalties |

## 5. Leasing Services

### Three Leasing Models

1. **Hands-On Leasing** (Included)
   - Owner manages process themselves
   - Access to marketing syndication and screening tools

2. **Agent Connection Service**
   - Hemlane connects owner with vetted local agent
   - Agent handles showings
   - Platform handles marketing and screening

3. **Tenant Placement Package** (Full Service)
   - Hemlane in-house team manages entire process
   - Digital lockbox for self-showings
   - Biometric + ID verification for tours
   - Average placement: **19 days**

### Premium Listing Feature
- Boosts listings on Redfin, Oodle, and partner sites
- **Doubles volume of incoming leads**

## 7. Maintenance & Repair

### In-House Coordination Team
- **NOT outsourced** (unlike competitors)
- **2-hour response time** requirement

- **10% of tickets resolved via troubleshooting** (no dispatch needed)
- **No markups** on vendor invoices

### Maintenance Controls

- Full visibility into all communications

### Vendor Network

- Can add preferred vendors

## 8. Eviction Shield

Optional add-on: **$4.95/unit/month**

Three-step process:
1. **Mediation**: Contact tenant, create payment plan (resolves 90% of cases)
2. **Legal Notices**: Local process server delivers three notices
3. **Attorney Connection**: Connect with local attorney for formal proceedings

Described as "Apple Care for your rental."
