## 🆕 Unique Skills (New to Registry)

### 1. SKILL-215: rent-rewards-program

**Priority**: P1
**Category**: consumer-loyalty

**Description**: 
Points-based rewards system that allows members to earn points on rent payments (their largest monthly expense).

**How It Works**:
- **Alliance Properties**: Direct integration via PMS, automatic points
- **BillPay**: Any property can participate, members pay through BILT
- **No Fees**: Free when paying from bank account

**Value Proposition**:
- Rent is largest expense for most Americans
- Previously "dead money" - no rewards possible
- BILT turns rent into rewards like credit card spend

**Key Differentiator**: **ONLY** platform that rewards rent at scale.

### 2. SKILL-216: credit-boost-rent-reporting

**Priority**: P0
**Category**: consumer-loyalty

**Description**: 
Free rent reporting to all three credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion) to help members build credit.

**Features**:
- **All Three Bureaus**: Complete coverage
- **Free**: No cost to members
- **Automatic**: On-time payments reported
- **Opt-In**: Members choose to participate

**Why Critical**:
- 45M+ Americans are "credit invisible"
- Rent is often largest expense but traditionally doesn't build credit
- Can significantly impact credit scores

**Key Differentiator**: Most competitors charge for rent reporting. BILT is FREE.
