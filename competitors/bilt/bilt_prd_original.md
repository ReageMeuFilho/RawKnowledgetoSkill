## 1. Executive Summary

## 2. The Three-Sided Marketplace

The symbiotic relationship between three key stakeholders:

| Stakeholder | Role in Ecosystem | Value Received |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Renters (4M+ members)** | Pay rent, spend in neighborhood, engage with platform | Earn rewards on largest expense, build credit, access exclusive benefits |
| **Property Managers (4.5M+ homes)** | Provide payment infrastructure, offer lease incentives | Up to 3x increase in early payments, 20% reduction in acquisition costs, new revenue streams |
| **Merchants (50,000+)** | Offer rewards and benefits to members | Access to highly engaged, localized customer base within 15-mile radius |

### The Flywheel Effect

The self-reinforcing flywheel that drives Bilt's growth:
- More properties join → More residents gain access
- Larger resident base → More spending in local neighborhoods
- More neighborhood spend → Attracts more merchants
- Richer merchant network → More value to residents AND revenue share for properties
- More value → Incentivizes additional properties to join

## 3. The Rent-to-Own Lifecycle

Bilt aims to be a lifelong financial partner:

**Phase 1: Renting** - Entry point where members earn points on rent payments, build credit through free rent reporting, and enjoy neighborhood benefits.

**Phase 2: Home Buying** - Members can redeem Bilt Points toward a down payment—one of the most unique redemption options in the industry.

**Phase 3: Home Owning** - Future state with Bilt Card 2.0 where members earn points on mortgage payments (partnership with United Wholesale Mortgages).

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 Rent Payments & Rewards

**Alliance Properties**: Direct integration with property management systems (RealPage, Yardi, MRI Software, AppFolio, Buildium). Members pay through resident portal, earn points automatically.

**BillPay Service**: For non-Alliance properties, members can still earn points on rent at any home through the BillPay service.

**Points Earning**: Members earn Bilt Points on their largest monthly expense—rent—with no fees when paying from a bank account.

### 4.2 Credit Building (Credit Boost)

**Free Rent Reporting**: On-time rent payments reported to all three major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion) at no cost. This helps members build credit history using their largest recurring expense.

### 4.3 Status Tiers

| Tier | Requirements | Benefits |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Blue** | Entry level | Core earning and redemption |
| **Silver** | Activity thresholds | Enhanced benefits |
| **Gold** | Higher activity | Premium benefits including Home Away from Home |
